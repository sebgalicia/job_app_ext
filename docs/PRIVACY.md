# Privacy

Job Autofill does not collect or transmit personal data. It makes no network requests for matching and uses no analytics or remote AI service.

Imported answers, matching preferences, site settings, detected or edited company and role context, and opt-in tracked application records are stored locally with the browser's `browser.storage.local` API. When you focus a supported field, the extension reads that field's question and available options to create a local preview. It does not send the page content or your answers to a server.

The optional job tracker stores application page URLs along with the company, role, and save date on this device. You can clear extension data through Firefox's storage controls or remove the extension.
